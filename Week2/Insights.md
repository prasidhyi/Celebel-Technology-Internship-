# Business Insights

1. Delivered orders generated the highest revenue among all order statuses.

2. Electronics products had the highest average selling price.

3. Premium-priced products contribute significantly to the overall revenue.

4. Foreign key constraints help maintain consistency between customers, orders, and products.

5. Aggregation functions like SUM, COUNT, and AVG make it easy to analyze sales performance.

6. JOIN operations combine information from multiple tables to generate meaningful business reports.
