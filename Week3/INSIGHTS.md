# Business Insights

1. Customers with higher total sales can be identified using aggregation and ranking functions.

2. Window functions such as `RANK()` and `ROW_NUMBER()` make it easy to compare customer performance without grouping the entire result.

3. Common Table Expressions (CTEs) improve query readability by breaking complex SQL queries into smaller logical parts.

4. Subqueries help answer business questions such as identifying orders above the average sales value and finding the highest order placed by each customer.

5. Combining JOINs, CTEs, and Window Functions provides meaningful business reports such as customer rankings based on total sales.

6. SQL analytical functions can be used to identify top customers, customers with only one order, and customers whose sales are above the overall average.
